Campaign Days

A website refresh that shows the people who work there. For your firm, a campaign day can be a website relaunch: the people and offices on your new pages, photographed on one planned day. It is one shoot built around one idea and planned with you before the quote.

On the Day

A campaign day starts in week 2, once all plans have been approved. Our full team of photographers and assistants works through the plan on site, for one full day. You prepare a setting for our team and let us work.

Where Each Picture Runs

Before the shoot day we agree where each picture will run, such as a wide banner on the home page or a phone screen. A picture framed for a phone crops badly in a wide banner, with the person at the edge and no room for a headline. Each picture is framed for the place it will run.

Rights

You set your rights as the first line of defence, and protection to your digital assets.
