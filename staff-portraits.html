How it starts

One backdrop for everyone

Portraits taken a few at a time, in whichever room had a free wall, make a team page that reads as several firms. One has a window on the left, the next has a grey wall, and the crop sits at the chest on one and the shoulders on another. One backdrop and one light setup at your offices gives every person the same frame.

About 20 minutes each

Each person is booked for one slot of about 20 minutes. The first frames of a portrait come out stiff, and 20 minutes leaves time to get past them. Everyone gets one retouched portrait and a tight crop for the web, and leaders can add a few more frames.

After the day

Proofs of your own people arrive in three business days, before finals are made. Finals arrive seven business days after you choose, in a private gallery. We keep the copyright and you get a licence to use the portraits on your website, recruiting pages, press and internal channels.

Not included

Raw files are not included. Paid advertising use is a separate line in the quote.
