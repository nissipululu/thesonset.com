Monthly Content

Monthly Content runs the AI system for your business.

In the post AI era what people value moves to media that is real and well directed. We set up an AI system that edits and publishes your photos and videos and keeps your files organized, and we make the real shoots it starts from.

01

Who

Small local businesses, brands and online shops, plus schools, hospitals and government. One person on your side approves.

02

What

One planned filming day a month gives us the real photos and video. We edit them with AI tools, write the captions and publish each post to your own accounts once you approve it.

Every photo and video is filed in a library you own, named one way and sorted by what it shows. We also work on site with your teams on campaign days.

03

When

First the plan, then the setup, then every month the shoot, your approved posts and a System Review. It runs as a setup, then a monthly care plan.

04

Why

So you stop making, posting and sorting by hand, and the photos and video people see are real.

05

Payments, Terms & Quotes

Monthly Content has a setup, then a monthly care plan. Ending it takes 30 days written notice. The written quote comes after the plan and says what you pay and when.

06

Not included

We do not run your ads or add extra filming days. Strategy beyond the monthly System Review is not part of it. Email hello@thesonset.com if you have any other questions.
