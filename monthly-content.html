Partner thought leadership

Videos and photos of your partners, made in your offices and edited for your website and social channels. The filming day is set in advance for each month. Your partners can plan around it.

Recruiting content

Photos and videos of the people who work at your firm, for your recruiting pages. Students, associates and laterals see who they would work with, in place of stock images.

Who it suits

Firms and groups that want regular photos and videos and have nobody on staff whose job is video.

  • Law, accounting, advisory and wealth firms

    Partners and advisors photographed and filmed in your own offices, with one person at your firm approving every piece.

  • Dental and clinic groups with several practices

    We film each practice's team and rooms on a day that fits the appointment book, so every location has current photos and videos for its page and for hiring.

  • Property managers, hospitality groups and online brands

    The same monthly day, set around your buildings, rooms or products and the people who work there.

A month in four steps

  1. 01

    Plan

    We talk, then you receive a plan on one page of what we will make that month.

  2. 02

    Film

    One planned filming day at your offices.

  3. 03

    Edit

    We edit the day into finished videos and photos, and Nissi checks every cut.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Edited videos and photos arrive on the date agreed up front, followed by a review call.

Files and where you can use them

Edited videos and photos for your own channels. How many of each you receive is set after your first month, once we have seen what one filming day at your offices produces. You can use the work on your website, social channels and email, and inside your firm. We keep the copyright and you get a licence. Paid advertising use is named in the quote.

One approver and one day a month

One person at your firm approves the work. A cut sent to four people for comments comes back as four different edits, so each cut goes to that one person. We also need access to your offices for one day a month.

Term, payment and the quote

Monthly content starts with three months, then runs month to month. The first month is paid before the filming day, and ending it takes 30 days written notice. The written quote comes after the plan and tells you how much.

Not included

We do not post or schedule for you, manage your ads or add extra filming days. Strategy beyond the monthly review call is not part of it.
