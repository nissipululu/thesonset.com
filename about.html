I'm Nissi Pululu

A Commercial Photographer based in Toronto, and the founder of Sonset Inc, an agency built on the belief that great visuals represent you.

My work lives at the intersection of photography and brand strategy. Over the past several years I've directed commercial campaigns and developed visual identities for everyone from emerging artists to international names like DVSN and Davido, corporate clients like GFL Toronto and Toronto Community Housing, and cultural institutions like Manifesto Toronto.

Sonset isn't a studio. It's a creative system that develops artists, businesses and brands to figure out who they are visually, and leave with the assets to prove it. If you're ready to build something worth seeing, let's talk, reach out and let's make it happen.
