Five steps from the first email to the first day

  1. 01

    You email

    Write to nissi@thesonset.com. A few lines about your firm and what you want made are enough.

  2. 02

    A 15 minute call

    We ask what your firm posts today, who approves it and what you need next.

  3. 03

    A plan on one page

    It shows what we would make for your firm, built from what you post today. Forward it to anyone who needs to see it before you decide.

  4. 04

    A written quote

    It comes after the plan. It says what you get, where you can use it and what is not included.

  5. 05

    The first day

    We come to your offices on the day we agree. For monthly content, the first month is paid before the filming day.

A smaller first step

A portrait day lets you judge the work before you commit to a month. It covers up to 20 people at about 20 minutes each, and proofs arrive in three business days, before finals.
