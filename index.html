Who does what

Your marketing lead and your agency keep their roles. We plan the day and make the photos and videos, and you post them when you decide. Posting, ad management and strategy beyond the monthly review call are not part of the work.

Questions before you write

Is there published work for other firms?

Judge us on the plan: it is built from what your firm posts today and reaches you before any quote. We have no published work for firms yet.

How much partner time does this take?

On a portrait day each person is booked for one slot of about 20 minutes. Monthly content is one filming day a month. One person at your firm approves the work.

How much is it?

The written quote tells you how much. It comes after the plan and before anything is booked.

Are there extra charges?

The written quote says what you get, where you can use it and what is not included. Raw files are not included, and paid advertising use is a separate line.

Who owns the files?

We keep the copyright and you get a licence. The quote spells out where it applies, and portraits can be used on your website, recruiting pages, press and internal channels.

How long is the monthly commitment?

Three months to start, then month to month. Ending it takes 30 days written notice, and the first month is paid before the filming day.
